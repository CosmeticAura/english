# A Temporary Matter study loop

For the one word quiz on "A Temporary Matter" by Jhumpa Lahiri. On quiz day you get one word and a quote that uses it, and you explain why and how the word and its imagery matter to the story.

Live site: https://cosmeticaura.github.io/english/

Five study loops: the story night by night, the CLIMB answer formula, then the three groups every word falls into (light and dark, home and food, secrets and the ending). Each loop goes read, a 2 min 30 s break, flashcards, another break, a brain dump, then mark what you missed. Progress saves in your browser only.

`mock/` is a mock test like the real quiz: get a random word and the moment it comes from, write your answer (10 minute timer if you want it), then check it against a 5 point checklist and a model answer. Twelve words in all.

The old Interpreter of Maladies site is kept on the branch `interpreter-of-maladies-2026-11-02`.
