# English study loops

Study loops for the Jhumpa Lahiri stories from Interpreter of Maladies, ELA 10th grade at The Clinton School.

Live site: https://cosmeticaura.github.io/english/

- `mr-pirzada/`: "When Mr. Pirzada Came to Dine": the story start to end, the 1971 history, the CLIMB answer formula, symbols and key words, themes.
- `a-temporary-matter/`: "A Temporary Matter": the five nights, the CLIMB formula, and the three word groups.
- `mock/`: the A Temporary Matter mock test.

Each loop goes read, a 2 min 30 s break, flashcards or multiple choice, another break, a brain dump, then mark what you missed. Progress saves in your browser only.

The old Interpreter of Maladies site is kept on the branch `interpreter-of-maladies-2026-11-02`.
